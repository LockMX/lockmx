# Returns, withdrawal right and legal guarantee

The return policy delivered by the client (`apps/web/src/server/site/policies/return-policy.*.ts`, page `/returns`, last updated 2026-11-16), compared on 2026-11-17 with the law on distance sales to consumers and on the conformity of goods. It is a technical record for the owner to take to the client, not legal advice. The policy has to be changed; the new text is the client's, reviewed by whoever handles the legal side of the business.

## Sources

Read on 2026-11-17 through an agent, in the consolidated texts of the Procuradoria-Geral Regional de Lisboa. The tool limited verbatim quotes to short excerpts, so the article numbers and the excerpts below are reliable, the rest is a paraphrase. The final wording is confirmed in the Diário da República before it is copied into a legal text (rule of `README.md`).

- DL n.º 24/2014, of 14 February (distance and off-premises contracts): https://www.pgdlisboa.pt/leis/lei_mostra_articulado.php?nid=2062&tabela=leis . Latest version listed: Lei n.º 10/2023, of 3 March. The annex (part A, model information; part B, model withdrawal form) is referred to by arts. 4.º and 11.º but was not read.
- DL n.º 84/2021, of 18 October (consumer rights in the sale of goods): https://www.pgdlisboa.pt/leis/lei_mostra_articulado.php?nid=3471&tabela=leis . The page says "O diploma ainda não sofreu alterações". Arts. 43.º and 51.º were only seen through a summary of the page: marked "not verified" below.

## Discrepancies

| # | The policy says | The law says | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Returns must be postmarked within 15 days of the purchase date. | The consumer has 14 days to withdraw, counted for goods from the day they acquire physical possession of them, not from the purchase. The period is met when the declaration is sent before it ends (art. 11.º n.º 3); the goods are then returned within 14 days of communicating the decision (art. 13.º n.º 1). Counting from the purchase can give less than the legal period when delivery takes more than a day. A longer period may be agreed (art. 10.º n.º 4). | DL 24/2014, arts. 10.º, 11.º, 13.º |
| 2 | The policy does not name the right of withdrawal, its period or how to exercise it, and gives no withdrawal form. | The supplier must inform of "a existência do direito de livre resolução do contrato, o respetivo prazo e o procedimento para o exercício do direito", with the form of part B of the annex (art. 4.º n.º 1 m)). Without that information the period is extended by 12 months (art. 10.º n.º 2). The supplier bears the burden of proving it informed (art. 4.º n.º 8). | DL 24/2014, arts. 4.º, 10.º |
| 3 | To return an item the customer must email `returns@` to obtain an RMA number. | The right is exercised with the form or "qualquer outra declaração inequívoca de resolução do contrato" (art. 11.º n.º 1), such as a letter, a phone call or the return of the goods (n.º 2). An RMA can be offered as a convenience, not required as a condition. Clauses that penalise the consumer for withdrawing are null (art. 11.º n.º 7). | DL 24/2014, art. 11.º |
| 4 | Returned items must be new and unused, with all tags and labels attached. | The consumer may inspect the goods as they would in a shop (art. 14.º n.º 1). If the handling went beyond that, the consumer answers for the loss of value, not for the loss of the right (n.º 2), and never when they were not informed of the right (n.º 3). The condition of the goods cannot be a reason to refuse the withdrawal. | DL 24/2014, art. 14.º |
| 5 | Return for "a refund, store credit, or an exchange". | Withdrawal gives a refund of every payment received, including the delivery costs (art. 12.º n.º 1), by the same means of payment unless the consumer expressly agrees otherwise, at no cost to them (n.º 2). Store credit or an exchange only if the consumer chooses it. The policy does not say the delivery costs are refunded (except the extra cost of a delivery more expensive than the standard one, n.º 3). | DL 24/2014, art. 12.º |
| 6 | "Please allow at least eight (8) days from the receipt of your item to process your return." | The refund is due within 14 days of being informed of the decision to withdraw (art. 12.º n.º 1). It may be withheld only until the goods are received or the consumer proves they were sent (n.º 4). A late refund is due in double, within 15 working days (n.º 6). "At least eight days" sets no limit. | DL 24/2014, art. 12.º |
| 7 | The customer pays all return shipping. | Allowed: the consumer bears the cost of returning the goods (art. 13.º n.º 2), unless the supplier agreed to bear it or did not inform them beforehand. The information is due before the contract (art. 4.º n.º 1 n)); for goods that cannot normally be returned by post, the agent's reading says the cost must be stated, to confirm in the text. If the information is missing, the consumer pays nothing (art. 4.º n.º 5). | DL 24/2014, arts. 4.º, 13.º |
| 8 | "Exceptions": for defective or damaged products, contact us to arrange a refund or exchange. | The legal guarantee is separate from the withdrawal right and the policy does not mention it. The seller answers for any lack of conformity that appears within three years of delivery (art. 12.º n.º 1); within two years it is presumed to have existed at delivery (art. 13.º n.º 1). The consumer chooses between repair and replacement, and may ask for a price reduction or resolution under the conditions of art. 15.º. Within 30 days of delivery the consumer may ask for immediate replacement or resolution (art. 16.º). Repair or replacement takes at most 30 days (art. 18.º n.º 3), at the seller's expense (n.º 1). On resolution the goods are returned at the seller's expense (art. 20.º n.º 4 a)) and the price is refunded within 14 days (n.º 6). The general sentence that the customer pays return shipping must not apply to defective goods. Not verified: a clause that excludes or limits these rights is null (art. 51.º), and a commercial guarantee must state that it does not affect them (art. 43.º). | DL 84/2021, arts. 12.º, 13.º, 15.º, 16.º, 18.º, 20.º |
| 9 | No exception to the withdrawal right is stated. | Goods made to the consumer's specifications or clearly personalised have no withdrawal right (art. 17.º n.º 1 c)), and the consumer must be told so before buying (art. 4.º n.º 1 p)). To ask the client: is any product (racks fitted to a van, for example) made to measure? | DL 24/2014, arts. 4.º, 17.º |

## Already changed in the site's texts (owner, 2026-11-17)

- The terms (section 7) link to `/returns` instead of the old `http://www.lockmx.com/return-policy`.
- Payments: every payment is processed by Shopify, which shows the available methods at checkout. The terms (section 6) say so instead of listing Visa, Mastercard, "mbway" and "shopify"; the stray "MBWAY" line of the privacy policy is gone; the return policy says refunds go back through Shopify to the method used, instead of "1-2 billing cycles" on a credit card. Per item 5, the refund by the same means of payment is also the legal rule.
