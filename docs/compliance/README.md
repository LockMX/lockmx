# Compliance

Legal and regulatory requirements that the site and the shop must meet, each with the source consulted and the date. It is a technical record to guide the work, not legal advice. Before launch the texts are reviewed by whoever is responsible for the legal side of the client's business.

## Rules

- Every requirement names the article and the source, and states how it was verified.
- Sources are the consolidated texts published by the Procuradoria-Geral Distrital de Lisboa (`pgdlisboa.pt`) when the Diário da República site (`diariodarepublica.pt`) could not be read by the tooling. The final wording is confirmed in the Diário da República before it is copied into the site or the legal texts.
- What could not be confirmed is listed as open, never filled in.

## Files

- `site-information-and-complaints.md`: information the site must show (identification and contacts), the Complaints Book, consumer dispute resolution, and the EU dispute resolution platform that no longer exists.
